Data Analytics Portfolio | Square Dance Attendance Trends

Welcome! I am a square dance caller and data analyst candidate completing the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate. For my capstone project, I combined my professional background in square dancing with data science to explore attendance trends for the National Square Dance Convention (NSDC).

My analysis draws from 74 years of historical attendance records and specific geographic registration data from the 74th and upcoming 75th conventions. By collaborating directly with the 75th NSDC Committee, I obtained exclusive CSV datasets to uncover deeper insights into the future of the activity.

Capstone Project Case Studies

  • Historical & 2025 Registration Analysis R Programming
    An exploratory data analysis of historical NSDC registration records available online.
  • The 75th National Convention (2026) Preview R Programming
    Using primary data sourced from the convention committee, this report cleans, combines and looks at current registration for the 2026 convention.
  • NSDC Attendance Dashboard Tableau
    An interactive visualization that maps registration data geographically.

Data sources: NSDC NEC, 74th NSDC, 75th NSDC, and the 75th NSDC Committee.
