Welcome to My Capstone Project

My name is Jennie Kirk, and I created this space to share analysis I have been developing as part of the Coursera Google Data Analytics certificate program. For the capstone project, learners were instructed to either use a provided sample dataset or identify and analyze a dataset of their own choosing. I am a square dance caller who teaches lessons in my hometown, which led me to explore attendance trends for the National Square Dance Convention. Overall attendance numbers for the past 74 years are available here. In addition, attendance counts by country—and by state for attendees within the United States—are available for the 74th convention here.
